# Synthetic evidence and proof boundaries

Every organizational identity, host, account, incident, address and event is **fictional**. All CSV exhibits emulate different log sources but were authored from a single simulated case, not independently collected from production. Their agreement is not external corroboration.

The two policy specimens contain genuine text bytes *within the exercise*, whose SHA-256 values can be computed and compared. Matching hashes establish integrity of delivered bytes, not a real-world original acquisition or evidence of secure boot.

The label X25519MLKEM768 is **metadata for a proposed key-exchange configuration**, not a demonstrated ML-KEM operation. Certificate-signature metadata is also not a demonstration of key-exchange negotiation. No genuine handshake or exfiltration has been authenticated by this exercise.

Sensor-specific clock correction follows recorded_utc = actual_utc + offset_seconds. Apply the edge-sensor offset only to its observations; the host audit source is not shifted.

This small edition deliberately excludes the broader research project, full network captures, production cryptographic software and confidential instructor materials. It is defensive training only.
